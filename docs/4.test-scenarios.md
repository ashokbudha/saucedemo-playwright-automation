# 4. Test Scenarios

## 4.1 Purpose

The purpose of the test scenarios is to define the high-level functionality that will be evaluated during testing of the SauceDemo application.

These scenarios provide a testing map for developing detailed test cases. They focus on the application's key functionality without defining detailed test steps or implementation-specific automation.

The scenarios will later be expanded into detailed test cases, which will then be evaluated to determine whether they should be automated, kept manual, or tested at another appropriate level.

## 4.3 Scenario Coverage

### TS-001 — Verify Authentication Functionality

Verify that users can authenticate appropriately using the predefined user accounts and that the application handles different authentication conditions correctly.

Coverage includes:

* Valid login
* Invalid credentials
* Locked-out user behavior
* Other predefined user behaviors
* Logout-related authentication state

### TS-002 — Verify Product Catalog and Product Information

Verify that the product catalog displays the expected products and that relevant product information is available.

Coverage includes:

* Product listing
* Product names
* Product descriptions
* Product prices
* Product images
* Product detail pages
* Navigation between product listing and product details

### TS-003 — Verify Product Sorting Functionality

Verify that the available product sorting options correctly arrange products according to the selected sorting method.

Coverage includes:

* Name: A to Z
* Name: Z to A
* Price: Low to High
* Price: High to Low

### TS-004 — Verify Shopping Cart Functionality

Verify that users can manage products in the shopping cart correctly.

Coverage includes:

* Adding products to the cart
* Removing products from the cart
* Viewing cart contents
* Verifying product information within the cart
* Verifying cart state after relevant actions

The exploratory finding regarding the absence of quantity controls will also be considered when developing detailed cart test cases.

### TS-005 — Verify Checkout Functionality

Verify that users can proceed through the checkout process and that required checkout information is handled correctly.

Coverage includes:

* Accessing checkout
* Entering required information
* Validation of required fields
* Reviewing checkout information
* Verifying order summary and totals

### TS-006 — Verify Order Placement Functionality

Verify that users can successfully complete the order placement process and receive the appropriate confirmation.

Coverage includes:

* Placing a valid order
* Order confirmation
* Order information
* Behavior when the cart contains no products
* Order identifier behavior

The exploratory findings related to empty-cart orders and order identifiers will be considered during detailed test-case development.

### TS-007 — Verify Navigation and Application State Functionality

Verify that the application's navigation controls and relevant application-state functions behave correctly.

Coverage includes:

* All Items navigation
* About navigation
* Logout
* Reset App State
* Application state after navigation or reset actions

The exploratory finding regarding the product button remaining in the **“Remove”** state after Reset App State will be considered during detailed testing.

### TS-008 — Verify Footer Links and Information

Verify that the footer displays the expected information and that its available links behave appropriately.

Coverage includes:

* Footer visibility
* Copyright information
* Twitter link
* Facebook link
* LinkedIn link

---



## 4.4 Next Step

The high-level scenarios will be expanded into detailed test cases. Each test case will contain the necessary information to execute and verify a specific behavior, including preconditions, test data, steps, and expected results.

After the detailed test cases are prepared, they will be evaluated for automation suitability.
